Privacy and security

Your trips live only on your device and in your iCloud

No account. We do not run a server of our own and cannot see your data. You can view and edit without a network.

Read more

Privacy and data security: We do not run a server of our own and cannot see your trips. Your data is only on your device and in iCloud. Backup & Sync and trip co-editing both use iCloud.

Files and images are also only on your device and in iCloud. You can export a backup file, or use iCloud Backup & Sync.

Works offline: Without a network, you can view and edit all your data. When iCloud Backup & Sync or co-editing is on, changes sync after you connect to a network.

Home

Home cards, arranged by you

Live Activity, to-dos, currency and overview. Touch and hold to move.

  • The cover shows the countdown to departure, and the day number during the trip
  • Press and hold to edit, then drag a card to a new spot
  • Cards can have several pages. Swipe sideways to switch

Home cards

Home holds 14 cards

Touch and hold to add a card, move a card or change its size. Each trip has its own layout. Tap a card to preview it on Home.

  • The Overview note of this trip.

  • Daily weather for this trip, for the trip place of each day. Within 10 days before departure, it is a forecast. Further out, it is the average of past years. Tap a day for details.

  • Steps, walking distance and flights climbed for each day of the trip. Read from Apple Health. Stays on your device.

  • Current event, countdown and next stop during a trip. Same as the Live Activity on the Lock Screen.

  • The current event, the next stop and the number of stops left today.

  • Files to show today, and files to carry throughout the trip.

  • Open to-dos. Tick them off here.

  • Items to buy and the stop to buy them at. Tick them off here.

  • Shows the last conversion. Without a network, it uses the last rate.

  • The departure date of the next trip, or the day of the current trip.

  • Shows one note on Home.

  • The notes of one trip, most recently changed first.

  • The number of unread inspirations, and the latest few. Tap one to open it.

  • Opens the scanner for links, text or trip co-editing invites. Companions also see the scan history.

14 cards in small, medium and large. The free version has one page. Premium allows more pages.

01 · Calendar / Timeline

Today’s events and Legs

Events, free time and travel time share one timeline.

  • Drag to change the time slot
  • Drag an unscheduled event into a time slot
  • How long you stay, how much time is free
  • Switch between calendar and timeline anytime
Read more

Calendar view: Each event is a calendar event. Drag to move it to another time slot, or to change its start and end times.

Timeline view: Shows when and where you go each day, how long you stay and how long each leg takes.

02 · Overview

Your trip overview, a page a day

Event cards show a cover and to-dos. Legs appear between cards.

  • One page a day. Swipe sideways to change day
  • Event cards with covers. Tick to-dos right on the card
  • Legs are marked between the cards
Read more

Overview has one page for each day, and you swipe sideways to change day. Cards without a photo show a map or an icon, and each leg shows its travel time between cards. Unscheduled events and important to-dos appear on the Overview page.

03 · Inspiration

Share from other apps toinspiration

Share from Safari, Maps, social apps and Photos. Turn any item into an event.

  • Share web pages, maps, posts and photos to TripEZGo to save them
  • Title and preview image fill in on their own. No tidying needed
  • Unread and not-added items are marked
  • Save as an event, note, shopping item, file or map marker
Read more

Share in from other apps: In Safari, Google Maps, Instagram, Threads or another app, tap Share and select TripEZGo. The item is saved in Library › Inspiration.

Save first, decide later: You do not need to choose a day now. The title and preview image are added automatically. Items you have not seen are marked unread. To see items not yet added, filter by “Not added”.

Add to…: Tap “Add to…” to turn an inspiration into an event, a note, a shopping item, a file or a map marker.

04 · Map

The map shows each day’s route

Events join into a route. Shops along the way are marked on the map.

  • Daily routes and legs
  • Pin the places you want to go
  • Locations and open or closed status of stores in Japan
  • Walking directions
Read more

After you set a place for events, the map shows each day’s route and where the events are in relation to each other.

You can add markers for places you want to go. Switch to Nearby to list events and markers around you by distance. Locations and open or closed status of stores in Japan are built in.

Walking directions: select a place and tap Start Navigation.

05 · Plan events

Tickets and to-dos added to the event

Add tickets, booking confirmations and to-dos to an event. Tap a file to open it.

  • Search for a place, or just paste a Google Maps link
  • Photos, files and e-ticket links
  • Different start and end time zones, all-day and unscheduled events
  • To-dos to tick off before you leave
Read more

You can set these for each event:

  • Place: Search by name or address, or paste a Google Maps link
  • Type / title: When you enter a place, the title and type are filled in automatically
  • Files: Images from the Photos app, files from the Files app, or links (for example, an e-ticket)
  • Time: Start and end can be in different time zones (take off in Taipei, land in Okinawa). All-day events (a hotel stay). Unscheduled events (no time set).
  • To-dos: “remember to book a table,” for example

Cancel an event to keep it with a strikethrough. Delete it to remove it completely. Move it later or earlier, and the events after it move with it by X hours and Y minutes.

06 · Legs

Between two events, the leg time is estimated automatically

Between two events there is a leg. The map estimates how long it takes.

  • Walking, driving, cycling or transit, with the time estimated automatically
  • When the leg takes longer than the gap, it shows “Late”
  • Several departures, with a reminder for the nearest one on the day
Read more

You can set a leg between two events: walking, driving, cycling, transit or other. When the leg takes longer than the gap between the two events, it shows “Late”.

You can enter several transit departures in advance. During the trip, the app reminds you of the departure closest to now. If you miss one, you can switch to the next.

07 · Companion co-editing and location sharing

Companion co-editing and location sharing

Companions edit events, notes and files together. During the trip, you can share your location.

  • Send an invite link, or scan a QR code to join a trip
  • Companions’ locations appear on the map
  • Choose whether notes, files, to-dos and shopping lists are shared with companions
Read more

Companions can edit the same trip together. During the trip, turn on “Share my location” to show companions on the map with the time of their last update. Your location is shared only with companions of this trip. You can turn it off at any time.

Notes, files, to-dos and shopping lists can each be shared with companions or kept private.

Pricing was the thing I agonized over most. I built this to genuinely help people who travel on their own — but realistically, it has to earn something to keep going.

Backstory Why an iOS engineer who often travels solo built his own trip planner Read the story

How it started

On past trips I always had great companions who did the planning, and I mostly went with the flow. Then in September 2023 I took my first-ever solo trip — a one-day dash to Nagoya — and something switched on. Like people say: you either never do it, or you never stop.

Plenty more followed: 10 days solo in Kyushu, 6 days solo in Hiroshima and Okayama, 7 days solo through Shimane, Tottori, Himeji and Kobe, 10 days solo around Nagoya, Ise and Mie… and so on.

Planning before you leave, keeping on top of things once you are there — when you travel on your own, and especially solo, it is all on you. I needed a tool to keep track of all of it. I tried plenty of approaches, and none fit quite right. With AI everywhere, and being an iOS developer myself, I thought: why not build one?

Apple Notes / Obsidian / Notion / Google Docs

This is what I first planned trips with. The upside is that it is simple: open it and you are in a rich text editor, free to drop in event titles, images and files however you like.

The downside is that same freedom. There is no fixed format, so it is hard to see the whole trip at a glance. Once the plan gets long, scrolling to find things is painful; on the road there is no quick way to jump to where you are now; and when plans change, it is a real hassle. And there are no notifications, calendar, trip map or widgets.

In practice, I found it great for collecting places and sketching an outline — you can get a rough plan down fast. But for working out the details, or checking things mid-trip, it is not very handy.

Apple Calendar / Google Calendar

On my most recent trip, Okinawa on our own, we planned everything in Apple / Google Calendar. Planning is really easy: drag, drop, and the event's there; changing a time is just another drag. You can see the whole trip at a glance — where the gaps are, where you are staying too long. On the road you can set alerts, so you know right away how long you have got here and what is next, and last-minute changes are easy. Notifications and widgets work too.

The downside is that things end up scattered. There is no overview of your to-dos, files or notes, and no map view. A calendar is all about the event itself, and each event stands on its own. But some trip information cuts across events: train tickets, a souvenir list, notes. Or say I finish event A with plenty of time left — I want to know which events on other days are on the way and could move to today.

Other trip-planning apps

I also tried a few trip planners from big e-commerce groups. Each had standout features — an overview of your plans, adding events straight from a place, automatic scheduling, maps. But in use, they all felt geared more toward social and shopping. Fair enough: for a company, a product has to make money.

So TripEZGo exists: events, legs, maps and companion co-editing.
